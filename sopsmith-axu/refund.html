Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

14-day no-questions refund

If SOPsmith is not for you, email hello@sopsmith.app within 14 days of your initial purchase and we will refund it in full — no justification needed. This applies to monthly and annual plans alike.

How to request one

  • Email us from the address on your subscription with “Refund” in the subject line.
  • We confirm within 2 business days; refunds are issued through our payment processor (Paddle) and typically appear on your statement within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

Cancellation is not deletion

You can cancel a subscription at any time from your account page. Cancellation stops future billing; access continues until the end of the period you already paid for. If you cancel within the 14-day window and want your money back instead, request a refund as above.

Founding-member pricing

The founding-member rate applies for as long as your subscription stays active. If you cancel and later return, standard pricing applies.

Fair use

We reserve the right to decline a refund in cases of clear abuse — for example, generating a large volume of drafts and then requesting a refund. In those situations we will always contact you first.

Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is wrong, please email us before filing a chargeback with your bank — we answer within 2 business days and can usually resolve it faster than a bank can.
