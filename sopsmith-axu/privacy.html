Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what SOPsmith collects, why, and what you can do about it. It is written to be read, not to be skipped.

What we collect

  • Your email address — when you join the waitlist or create an account, to send product updates and service emails.
  • Draft content you enter — the structured answers and SOP drafts you create in the tool, so the service can generate and review your drafts.
  • Basic usage data — aggregate page views and feature usage, to understand what to improve. No advertising trackers, no cross-site profiling.
  • Billing information — handled entirely by our payment processor (Paddle). We never see or store your full card number.

What we never do

We do not sell your data, rent it, or share it with advertisers. We do not use your drafts to train public models. Your drafts are processed to serve you and are not reused for anyone else.

Who processes data

To run the service we rely on a small number of processors: our hosting provider (site delivery), our payment processor (billing and tax), and the AI model provider used to generate drafts. Each processes only what is needed to provide the service.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in, plus privacy-respecting, aggregate analytics. There is no third-party advertising cookie on this site.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, correction of it, or deletion of your account and drafts at any time by emailing hello@sopsmith.app. We respond within 30 days. EU/UK users additionally have the right to lodge a complaint with their supervisory authority.

Retention

Waitlist emails are kept until launch plus twelve months, then deleted. Account and draft data is kept while your account is active and deleted within 90 days of account deletion.

Children

SOPsmith is intended for university and graduate applicants and is not directed at children under 16.
